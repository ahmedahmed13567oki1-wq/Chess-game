Privacy Policy

Chess – Play Against Computer

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chess Game application handles user information.

The application is an offline chess game that allows users to play against the computer. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

The application does not require an internet connection, does not display advertisements, and does not require user registration or account creation.

2. Information We Collect

The application does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

We do not collect:

  • Names or email addresses.
  • Phone numbers or contact information.
  • Location data.
  • Personal files, photos, or media.
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes.

3. Game Data Handling

All chess gameplay takes place locally on the user's device.

The application does not transmit chess moves, game progress, or gameplay information to external servers.

If the application saves game settings or progress, this information remains stored locally on the device.

4. Permissions

The application does not require access to sensitive device permissions, such as:

  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Contacts.
  • Location.
  • Photos or media.

The game can be used without granting these permissions.

5. Third-Party Libraries

The application may use standard development libraries required for its operation.

The application does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking services that collect personal user information.

6. Sharing Information

We do not sell, share, or transfer users' personal information to third parties.

The application does not communicate gameplay data to external servers.

7. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Since the game does not collect personal information, users can enjoy the game without providing personal details.

8. User Control and Data Deletion

Users do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

Any locally stored game data or settings can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application from the device.

9. Google Play Data Safety

Based on the application's current functionality:

  • Data collected: None.
  • Data shared with third parties: None.
  • Advertising: None.
  • Account creation: Not required.
  • Internet connection: Not required for gameplay.
  • Data encryption in transit: Not applicable to offline gameplay.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in the application's features or functionality.

Any updates will be published on this page, along with the revised update date.
